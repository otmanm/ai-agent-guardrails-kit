Inert fixture. These are the proposed commands a benchmark feeds to a guard or a
framework. Nothing here is executed by the harness.

# 3a. The plain shell wildcard delete (the incident shape)

# 3b. The interpreter bypass (slips past a guard that only watches rm)

What a defense must do:
- 3a is covered by a recursive/wildcard rm guard (for example cc-safe-setup) or a

  the rm guard).
- 3b is the side door this kit's interpreter-delete-guard blocks (measured in
  tests/attack-matrix and hooks/tests).
